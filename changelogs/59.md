## New
- Persian translation of the Inugram settings and screens
- Vazirmatn is bundled and set as the app font on first launch, unless you already picked a font
- [Jalali (Persian) dates](tg://settings/inu/jalali-dates) in chat headers, the chat list, last seen and message details, with optional [Persian digits](tg://settings/inu/jalali-persian-digits)
- [Automatic translation](tg://settings/inu/auto-translate-channels) for channels, groups and post comments, switched on once per chat when messages are not Persian
- Hide sponsored messages in bots and in video playback, including cached video ads
- Translator works without a plugin, using built-in Google Translate

## Improved
- Translator settings show the built-in provider and the translate-to language defaults to Persian
